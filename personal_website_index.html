About

I grew up in Almaty navigating identity across languages and histories. That question—who gets remembered, who gets access, who gets erased—pushed me into building things that make systems more fair and more usable.

It’s why I digitized deportation records with the Volga German Institute, and why I’m drawn to applied AI: if a model can change a decision in the real world, it should be accountable, testable, and built for the people who need it most.
