Research

Here are a few dirrections I’ve been exploring:

EmojiPrompt decoding (WashU, Prof. Vorobeychik)

I worked on turning emoji-based obfuscation into something we can actually evaluate and break down: building datasets, training sequence-to-sequence models, and stress-testing where the method fails in high-stakes settings.

Focus: privacy • LLM safety • evaluation • seq2seq modeling

Medical cohort demographics pipeline (MIT Critical Data Lab, Prof. Leo Celi)

I’m building a pipeline that extracts and standardizes “Table 1” demographics from medical papers so we can audit who gets represented in research (and where the blind spots are).

Focus: data extraction • standardization • scientific auditing

Volga German records database (Wichita State, VGI)

I helped consolidate and clean 17,000 deportee archival records into a searchable dataset for families and researchers. It’s the kind of work that feels quiet, but really matters.
